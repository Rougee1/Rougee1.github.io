Privacy Policy — Holo Boxing

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Version française

1. Who we are

Holo Boxing is published by Redoine Ben-Geloune, sole trader, 13 rue Jules Vallès, 60160 Montataire, France. Contact for any question about your data: rouge.studio.game@gmail.com.

The game never asks for your name, your email or an account.

2. What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (level, gear, cards, tokens, in-game money) and settings are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game without exporting your save deletes them.

Your boxing licence asks for your year of birth. It stays on your phone. Only the "under 16" indication is passed to Google's ad module, so that you are only shown non-personalized ads. It also turns off detailed statistics (sections 6 and 7).

3. What our server keeps

On first launch the game creates a random player ID, not linked to your name or your phone. Our server, hosted in the European Union (Google Cloud, in Belgium) whatever your country, keeps with this ID:

  • your gem balance and the history of the gems you earn and spend;
  • your purchases: product, date, order number, real or test purchase, and the link between two IDs if you restore your purchases on another save;
  • your Club Card: start and end of each period, and any refund;
  • the video rewards you received: placement, date and time;
  • the date of your last login, and your login days over the last 14 days, for the daily chests;
  • two technical fingerprints, of your phone's key and of your save code, which protect your save (the server never keeps the key or the code themselves);
  • the app version and type.

It uses them to keep your gem balance, verify your purchases and rewards, prevent cheating and restore your purchases. Your IP address necessarily reaches the server with each request, but we don't store it. This ID is never used for advertising or linked to our gameplay statistics.

Abuse protection. To check that requests come from the genuine app, the game uses Firebase App Check with Play Integrity, Google services that send Google a technical attestation of the app and the device.

4. Purchases

Payment is handled by Google Play: we never see your bank card. To verify a purchase we use RevenueCat, a provider acting on our behalf. It receives the Google Play purchase token, the product bought, the device type and operating system, your player ID and the date of your last visit. RevenueCat stores this data in the United States, with the contractual safeguards required by the GDPR.

5. Gameplay statistics (audience measurement)

To improve the game, Holo Boxing counts gameplay events: fight start and end, level reached, videos offered or watched, shop opened, and technical errors (a code and the screen involved, never the content of the error). These statistics:

  • are used only by us, the publisher, to measure audience, fix problems and test improvements;
  • use a random ID dedicated to statistics, renewed at least every 13 months, not linked to your advertising ID, your player ID or your purchases;
  • never include your IP address;
  • are hosted in the European Union and deleted after 25 months at most.

As allowed by the French data protection authority (CNIL) for this kind of measurement, they are on by default. You can object at any time by turning off Settings → Gameplay statistics.

6. Detailed statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only with your consent

If you accept in the consent window (16 and over only), the game also sends these events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service. It uses an app-instance ID, an approximate location derived from your masked IP address, the screens viewed, session length and purchases (product and price). It does not receive your advertising ID. We have opted out of Google using this data for its own products.

If you refuse, nothing is sent to Firebase. You can change your mind any time: Settings → Ad privacy reopens the consent window.

This window only exists in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. Everywhere else, nothing is sent to Firebase, since nobody asked for your agreement.

7. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional video ads: you choose to watch them to get a bonus. They are provided by Google AdMob, a Google service. To show these ads, measure them and fight fraud, Google's service automatically collects:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with the game: launches, taps, video views;
  • diagnostic information: launch time, hangs, energy use;
  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID.

Your choice, in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. A window asks on first launch whether you accept personalized ads. Refusing is as easy as accepting, and you can change your mind any time in Settings → Ad privacy. If you refuse, you keep the same videos and rewards, with non-personalized ads.

Everywhere else, there is no window: Google may show you personalized ads, under its own rules. To refuse them, delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID; the path can vary by phone). Your videos and rewards stay the same.

Everywhere, if you are under 16, you only see non-personalized ads (section 12).

More: how Google uses data from partner apps and Google Privacy Policy.

9. Who receives this data

  • Google: for AdMob, as our ad provider; for Firebase (server, App Check, and detailed statistics if you consented) and Google Cloud, as a provider acting on our behalf.
  • RevenueCat (United States): to verify purchases, as a provider acting on our behalf.

We never sell data. Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, with the GDPR safeguards described in their own policies.

10. How long

  • On your phone: while the game is installed.
  • On our server, while you play. After 3 years without any login:
    • if you never made a real purchase, your server save is deleted, with its gems;
    • if you made a real purchase, we keep your gem balance, your purchases and the list of one-time rewards you already received (no date or amount, so they aren't given twice) for as long as the game exists, so you find them when you come back, and we delete the rest. If the game were ever to close, we would tell you in advance.
  • Server technical logs, without IP addresses: 30 days.
  • Gameplay statistics: 25 months at most.
  • Detailed statistics (Firebase): data linked to your app-instance ID is kept for 2 months; anonymous overall reports remain afterwards.
  • Ad data: kept by Google under its own rules.
  • Emails you send us: while we handle your request, then deleted.

11. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, object to or restrict its use, ask for portability and withdraw your consent by writing to rouge.studio.game@gmail.com.

Since we don't know your name or email, include your player ID: it is shown in Settings → My save, with a "Copy" button. It lets us find your data on our server. Note: if we delete this data, your gem balance and purchases can no longer be restored.

These rights are the same wherever you live.

You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to the data protection authority of your country, if there is one.

12. Minors

Holo Boxing is meant for players 16 and over. If a player says they are under 16, wherever they live, the game doesn't ask for consent: it only shows non-personalized ads and sends nothing to Firebase.

13. Changes

If the game adds features that use data, this page will be updated before they ship, with a new date at the top.
